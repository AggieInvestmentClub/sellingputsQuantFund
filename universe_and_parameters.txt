UNIVERSE & INITIAL TEST PARAMETERS
Strategy: Bull put spreads after negative overreactions to non-earnings news
Status: v1 draft (starting values to backtest and revise, not final rules)
Source: "putSpread on negative overrreaction.pdf" + README + team review.
Items marked [PROPOSED] are starting values still open for debate.

======================================================================
1. TRADABLE UNIVERSE
======================================================================

1.1 Base list
  - US-listed common stocks with listed options (weeklies preferred)
  - Pool: S&P 500 constituents only, built POINT-IN-TIME
    (use membership as of each event date to avoid survivorship bias)
  - Hard market cap floor: $5B (1.3). Higher floors are tested in section 4.

1.2 Exclusions
  - ETFs, ETNs, leveraged products
  - Stocks with pending M&A / takeover deals (price pinned, binary risk)
  - Binary-event names where the "news" IS the fundamental (e.g. single-drug
    biotech FDA decisions, going-concern warnings, fraud findings)
  - Earnings date inside the trade window (entry date -> expiry)
  - Stocks under $20

1.3 Stock liquidity filters
  - Market cap               >= $5B
  - 20-day avg $ volume      >= $50M/day
  - 20-day avg share volume  >= 1M shares/day

1.4 Options liquidity filters
  - Open interest at short strike   >= 500 contracts
  - Open interest at long strike    >= 250 contracts
  - Avg daily options volume (name) >= 5,000 contracts
  - Short-leg bid-ask width         <= 10% of mid  (or <= $0.15, whichever is larger)
  - Expiry with 14-35 DTE must be listed

======================================================================
2. SIGNAL / EVENT DEFINITION (what counts as a "shock")
======================================================================

2.1 Price trigger
  - Beta-adjusted abnormal 1-day return (market-model residual):
        abnormal = stock return - beta * SPY return
        beta estimated over the prior 250 trading days, ending 5 days
        before the shock
  - Thresholds to test (cumulative):
        <= -5%, <= -10%, <= -15%
  - No volume requirement on the shock day
  - Skip if SPY falls more than 2% that day (market-wide selloff) [PROPOSED]

2.2 Earnings exclusion
  - No earnings release within +/- 3 trading days of the shock
  - No earnings release between entry and expiry

2.3 News categories to tag
  - Analyst downgrade
  - Peer / sector negative news (contagion)
  - Lawsuit filed
  - Regulatory probe
  - Short seller report
  - Product recall
  - Dilution / secondary offering
  - TBD (more categories to be added as we review the event set)
  - Other / unclassified (logged, not traded at first)

2.4 LLM filter ("is the reaction justified?")
  - Core test: estimated fundamental impact vs actual market cap lost
        market cap lost  = abnormal return * pre-shock market cap
        est. impact      = LLM's estimated $ hit to company value from the
                           news (e.g. lawsuit size, recall cost, dilution
                           amount, lost revenue * multiple)
        overreaction ratio = market cap lost / est. impact
    Example: company sued for $10M, small likely settlement, stock -10% on
    a $20B company ($2B lost) -> ratio = 200x -> overshot -> tradeable
  - Trade when overreaction ratio >= threshold (test: 2x, 3x, 5x)
  - LLM output: category, est. $ impact (low / mid / high), ratio,
    "justified / not justified / unsure", short reason
  - Trade only "not justified"
  - Backtesting: anonymize company name/ticker and dates in prompts; run a
    recognition test to check the model can't identify the company
    (look-ahead bias)

======================================================================
3. TRADE STRUCTURE (BASELINE)
======================================================================

3.1 Structure
  - Bull put spread only (sell higher-strike put, buy lower-strike put)

3.2 Short strike
  - Delta: 0.15, 0.20 (default), 0.25
  - Must also be below the post-announcement low

3.3 Long strike / spread width
  - Method A, by width: 2.5%, 5% (default), 7.5%, 10% of stock price,
    rounded to the nearest listed strike
  - Method B, by delta: long put at 0.05 or 0.10 delta
  - Test both methods

3.4 Minimum credit
  - Credit >= 25% of spread width (adjustable: 15%, 20%, 25%, 33%)

3.5 Entry (IV lag / selling-exhaustion trigger)
  - Theory: after the shock, selling cools and realized vol drops faster
    than implied vol. Sell premium while IV is still rich relative to
    what the stock is actually doing.
  - Earliest entry: 1 trading day after the shock
  - Enter on the first day all of these hold:
      a) IV / RV ratio >= threshold
           IV = 30-day ATM implied vol
           RV = realized vol over the days since the shock, excluding the
                shock day (min 1 day; switch to 3-day RV once available)
           threshold: 1.2, 1.5 (default), 2.0
      b) Selling has cooled: day volume <= 1.5x 20-day avg
         (test: 1.5x, 2x, off)
      c) No new post-announcement low that day
  - Max wait: 10 trading days after the shock, then skip the event

3.6 Profit target (dynamic, floor 50%)
  - Never take profit below 50% of max credit
  - Once >= 50% captured, close if profit is outpacing time decay:
        % of credit captured - % of DTE elapsed >= 20 points (test 10/20/30)
    e.g. 60% captured after 25% of the time -> close (stock bounced hard)
  - Otherwise keep holding until 75% captured or expiry week
  - Compare against fixed 50% / 75% targets and hold-to-expiry

3.7 Stop loss (ideas to test)
  - Credit multiple: close at 1.5x, 2x (default), 3x credit received
  - % of max loss: close at 50% / 75% of max spread loss
  - Price-based: underlying closes below the short strike
  - Price-based: underlying closes below the post-announcement low
  - Delta-based: short put delta rises above 0.50
  - News-based: new negative news on the same ticker -> LLM re-runs 2.4;
    close if the reaction now looks justified
  - None (hold to expiry, spread already caps loss)
  - No time stop

3.8 Max loss per trade
  - Adjustable: 1%, 2% (default), 3% of allocated capital

5. PORTFOLIO-LEVEL RISK
======================================================================
  - Exposure-review agent runs daily (and before each new entry):
      * Sum of max loss by GICS sector and industry
      * Beta-weighted delta by sector
      * Pairwise correlation of open positions (60-day returns)
      * Flags overexposure and recommends: skip next same-sector entry,
        size down, or prefer other-sector setups
  - Hard backstop in case the agent fails or lags [PROPOSED]:
      * Max 30% of total capital-at-risk in one sector
      * One position per ticker at a time
  - No portfolio drawdown rule
  - Assignment: if short leg goes ITM near expiry, close the spread rather
    than take shares (no stock holding in v0)

7. TESTING ORDER
======================================================================
  1. Build the event set: thousands of non-earnings shocks across the
     S&P 500, each tagged with a category. Measure beta-adjusted returns
     at +1 / +5 / +10 / +20 days per category to find which categories
     revert most. If nothing reverts, stop here.
  2. Set up LSEG data (options chains, IV history, news timestamps).
  3. Measure IV vs realized vol after shocks (is IV lagging?).
  4. Run the baseline spread (section 3) on the event set.
  5. Vary one parameter at a time from baseline, then a small grid on the
     2-3 parameters that matter most.
  6. Validate the chosen settings once on out-of-sample data.
  7. Paper trade live (Alpaca paper) before any real capital.

======================================================================
8. METRICS TO REPORT PER RUN
======================================================================
  - Number of trades, win rate, avg win / avg loss
  - Expectancy per trade ($ and % of max risk)
  - Total return, CAGR, max drawdown, Sharpe, Sortino
  - Return by news category and by entry lag
  - Abnormal returns at +1, +5, +10, +20 days (signal check, no options)
  - IV change from entry to exit vs realized vol (vol premium check)
  - Results with and without the LLM filter (does the LLM add value?)
